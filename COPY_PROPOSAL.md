The homepage structure is already trimmed (Hero, Story, Work, Services, Process with FAQ, Engagements, "More from Zqtion", closing call-to-action). This file proposes new wording only. **Nothing here is applied to the site yet.** It follows AGENTS.md: no invented clients, numbers, awards, prices or delivery times. Anything that needs a fact from you is marked **[NEEDS FACT]**.

## Principles

- Say what the visitor gets, not what the process is called. "Campaign films" beats "create the signal".
- Say the honesty rule once, clearly, on the Work page. Elsewhere, sell.
- Keep sentences short. Drop "X, not Y" constructions where a plain statement works.
