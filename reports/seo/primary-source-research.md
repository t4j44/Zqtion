# Primary-source research and methods

Reviewed 22 October 2026. Official engine guidance overrides speculative AEO/GEO tactics. No paid API, agent framework or runtime dependency was added.

## Search-engine guidance applied

- [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide), [AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): focus on useful original content, accessible text, internal discovery and truthful markup. Generative features have no separate technical eligibility contract. Google does not use llms.txt as a ranking shortcut. Neither markup nor citations can guarantee inclusion. These pages currently describe reporting differently; inspect the actual account for available generative-AI reports rather than promising a report UI.
- [Discussion forum](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/discussion-forum): public post authorship, dates and content must reflect the visible forum. [QAPage](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/qapage): one real question with visible answers; accepted answers require a real accepted relationship. [ProfilePage](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/profile-page): the profile's actual person is mainEntity. No invented people or ratings.
- [OpenAI crawlers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots): search crawling and training controls are independent. OAI-SearchBot supports search; GPTBot is a separate training policy. User-initiated retrieval is not equivalent to automatic indexing.
- [Anthropic bot policy](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler), [Perplexity crawlers](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers): distinguish search/retrieval from model development. Do not infer genuine-bot firewall access from a spoofed user agent.
- [Google robots guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots/intro): robots does not secure private records or reliably remove indexed URLs. Publicly reachable account shells use noindex; actual data protection remains Auth/RLS. Crawlers need render assets.
- [Search Console ownership](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080), [Bing Webmaster Tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/), [IndexNow documentation](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation): owner access and canonical hosting are prerequisites to reliable submission/measurement. Notifications do not promise indexing.
